# Sentinel AV — vulnerable-driver (BYOVD) blocklist.
#
# The most common way serious attackers reach the kernel today is not writing a
# rootkit from scratch but loading a legitimately signed BUT vulnerable driver
# and exploiting its bug (Bring Your Own Vulnerable Driver). The engine refuses
# the load of known-abusable signed drivers through the image-load and ELAM
# paths, mirroring Microsoft's own vulnerable-driver blocklist and extending it.
#
# Format: <sha256-hex>  <driver-name>  <reference>
# These are SHA-256 hashes of the driver IMAGE. An unexpected new kernel driver
# not on the allow path is held for scrutiny rather than trusted on its signature
# alone. Entries below are illustrative placeholders; the authoritative list is
# delivered (and extended from Microsoft's recommended block rules) through the
# signed update pipeline.

# <sha256>  <driver.sys>  <CVE / advisory>
